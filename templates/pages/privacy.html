PRIVACY POLICY

We respect your privacy.

ModernEstate is committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

01

Information We Collect

When you interact with ModernEstate, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Property preferences and requirements.
  • Messages and enquiries submitted through our website.
  • Information required to respond to your requests.
02

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information primarily to provide better property discovery and communication services.

  • Respond to property enquiries.
  • Contact you regarding your property requirements.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Provide relevant property information.
  • Send requested updates or communications.
03

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, comply with applicable law, protect our rights, or respond to legitimate requests.

04

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our website against unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

While we take appropriate precautions, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

05

Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website usage and provide a better user experience.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings.

06

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding the personal information we hold about you.

  • Request information about your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Withdraw certain communications or preferences.
07

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
